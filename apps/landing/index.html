How it works

From first scan to interview day, in four stops.

One local workspace carries each vacancy the whole way, from the moment it shows up in a scan to the prep pack you read before the interview.

  1. 01

    Scan

    One search covers Dutch and worldwide remote sources. Official ATS APIs and structured feeds come first, and page parsing is only used where nothing better exists.

  2. 02

    Check

    Roles in the Netherlands get a best-effort lookup on the IND public register of recognised sponsors. Other markets show that check as not available.

  3. 03

    Tailor

    Pick a CV and a vacancy. Your own Claude Code or Codex session drafts a tailored CV and letter, and your original file stays as it was.

  4. 04

    Track

    Saved jobs, applications, letters and interview prep share one local workspace that is still there the next time you open the app.

Employer verification, Netherlands

Sponsor checks that tell you what was checked.

For roles located in the Netherlands, the app looks the employer up by name on the IND public register of recognised sponsors. A name match is labelled as a possible match, because the legal entity behind a brand name can differ. Outside the Netherlands there is no equivalent register, and the app labels the check as not available.

  • A possible match shows the legal name and KVK number it matched on.
  • When no check ran, the card says so in plain words.

CV and letter tailoring

Tailored drafts from the AI you already pay for.

Open Vacancy Radar drives the Claude Code or Codex CLI that is already installed and signed in on your PC. The project never receives a password, token or API key. You choose the CV, the vacancy, the tone and the length, then edit the draft yourself.

  • Motivation letters, cover letters, recruiter messages and short application-form answers.
  • Your uploaded CV file is kept exactly as you uploaded it.
  • Prompts go to your chosen CLI's provider, under that provider's own terms.

Privacy

A map of where your data goes.

Open Vacancy Radar has no backend. This is the complete list of places data can travel, taken from the project's own privacy statement.

Your Windows PC

  • Open Vacancy RadarThe desktop app and its local runtime
  • workspace.dbYour CVs, applications, letters and settings
  • vacancy-engine.dbA cache of public job postings
  • Public job sources Search requests only. They carry nothing about you.
  • Your AI CLI's provider Only when you run an AI feature, through Claude Code (Anthropic) or Codex (OpenAI), under their terms.
  • Open Vacancy Radar servers There aren't any. The app has no account, sync service or analytics endpoint to talk to.
