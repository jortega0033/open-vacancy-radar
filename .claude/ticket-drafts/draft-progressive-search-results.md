## Goal
Show discovered vacancies incrementally as each discovery source finishes, instead of making the user stare at a "Scanning..." banner for the full scan duration (up to ~15 minutes worst case, see the Workable-global-discovery timeout budget) before seeing a single result.

## Why now / what already exists
User-reported UX pain: a single scan can legitimately take minutes, and the current `vacancy:run-scan` IPC channel is a single request/single response -- the renderer gets nothing until `runGlobalRemoteScan` fully resolves. `packages/vacancy-engine/src/pipeline/global-remote.ts`'s `runGlobalRemoteScan` already runs its independent phases in parallel (`Promise.all([runGlobalRemoteDiscovery(...), runOfficialGlobalRemoteSources(...), runWorkableGlobalDiscovery(...)])` -- the last one only just parallelized in this same session, previously ran sequentially after the other two and could add its own full duration on top), and `global-remote/discovery.ts`'s `runGlobalRemoteDiscovery` internally does the same for its own sub-sources (himalayas, jobicy, structured, feeds, jobtech, additional, keyed -- one `Promise.all` of 7). So the underlying work already completes source-by-source with real, individually-awaitable completion points; nothing about the *discovery* side is a monolithic black box. What is missing is any mechanism to observe those completions from outside the single `await` in `runGlobalRemoteScan`, and any IPC path for the main process to push a partial update to the renderer mid-scan (Electron's `ipcMain.handle`/`ipcRenderer.invoke` pairing is strictly one request -> one response; a push needs `webContents.send` on a separate channel the renderer subscribes to, the same shape `agentDock`'s `onSessionEvent` streaming already uses elsewhere in this codebase for AI runs).

## Scope
- **Engine side** (`packages/vacancy-engine`): give `runGlobalRemoteScan` an optional progress callback (e.g. `onSourceCompleted(sourceId, partialVacancies)`), fired as each of the `Promise.all` members above resolves, not just at the very end. This is a plumbing change to existing parallel `await`s, not new discovery logic.
- **IPC boundary** (`apps/desktop/electron/main.ts` + `preload.ts`): a new push channel (e.g. `vacancy:scan-progress`) the `vacancy:run-scan` handler emits on via `webContents.send` as the engine's progress callback fires; a matching `vacancyRadar.onScanProgress(callback)` subscription in the bridge, mirroring `agentDock.onSessionEvent`'s existing pattern.
- **Renderer** (`SearchPage.tsx`): accumulate partial vacancies into the visible list as progress events arrive, converging to the final, complete `GlobalRemoteReport` once `runScan`'s own promise resolves (the final report stays the source of truth; partial data is provisional and gets replaced, not merged, once available -- avoids any risk of a stale partial vacancy surviving past a real removal/reclassification in the final pass).
- Sponsor-matching (`applyWorldwideSponsorMatches`) and profile scoring (`applyWorldwideProfileScores`) currently run once over the *entire* deduplicated `discoveryAudit` after all discovery finishes (see `global-remote.ts` lines ~352-364) -- decide explicitly whether partial/streamed rows show with no score/sponsor-match yet (honest "not yet scored" state, simplest) or whether scoring also runs incrementally per batch (more complex, touches the sponsor-match Wikidata lookups' current all-at-once shape). Recommend the former for v1.

## Non-goals
- No change to the final report's correctness or completeness -- streaming is purely a "show something sooner" affordance layered on top of the existing full-scan contract, never a way to skip or shortcut discovery/verification.
- No reordering/pagination redesign: partial results still flow through the same client-side `filterResults`/`sortResults`/pagination path already in `SearchPage.tsx`, just re-run against a growing list instead of a `null` starting point.
- Does not address the Workable-global source's own 15-minute timeout budget (`WORKABLE_GLOBAL_TIMEOUT_MS`) directly -- that stays as-is; this ticket makes waiting on it feel better, not shorter.

## Acceptance criteria
- On a fresh scan, at least one vacancy becomes visible before the scan's own promise resolves (provably before, via a test source that resolves slower than another).
- The final displayed list, once the scan completes, is identical to what a non-streaming scan would have shown -- no partial-data artifact survives into the final state.
- Navigating away and back (see this session's scan-reattachment fix in `SearchPage.tsx`) does not break or duplicate the progress subscription -- rejoining an in-flight scan should ideally also rejoin its progress stream, not just its final-completion poll.
- Existing `vacancy:run-scan` behavior (the request still ultimately resolves with the full report) is unchanged for any caller that ignores the new progress channel.

## Risk
Medium. Touches the IPC contract (new channel, new bridge method, both namespaces need test-surface updates like `test/ipc-sender-guard.test.ts`'s channel-count floor and `test/preload.test.ts`'s frozen key lists) and the engine's public scan entry point's signature. The main design risk is scope creep into "streaming scoring" (see the sponsor-match/profile-score question above) -- worth explicitly deciding "not yet scored" for v1 before starting, rather than discovering the complexity mid-implementation.
